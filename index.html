Quick-Revision Capsules

Anomalous configurations

Cr: [Ar]3d⁵4s¹ and Cu: [Ar]3d¹⁰4s¹ — half-filled & fully-filled d-subshells give extra stability (exchange energy).

Diagonal relationship

Li ↔ Mg, Be ↔ Al, B ↔ Si show similar properties due to comparable charge/radius ratio.

Lanthanoid contraction

Poor shielding by 4f electrons shrinks radii — so Zr (160 pm) and Hf (159 pm) are almost twins.

Inert pair effect

Heavier p-block elements prefer lower oxidation states: Tl⁺, Pb²⁺, Bi³⁺ are more stable.

Modern periodic law

Properties are periodic functions of atomic number (Moseley), not atomic mass (Mendeleev).

IUPAC naming (Z > 100)

0 nil, 1 un, 2 bi, 3 tri, 4 quad, 5 pent, 6 hex, 7 sept, 8 oct, 9 enn → Z=119 is Ununennium (Uue).
